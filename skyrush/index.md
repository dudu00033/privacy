# Privacy Policy: SKY RUSH: Jet Combat

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how the game SKY RUSH: Jet Combat ("the game") handles information. In it, "we" and "us" mean CG Art Studio, the game's developer. The game is a single-player game for Android and Windows, and it plays fully offline. The Android version shows ads when the device is online.

## Summary

- **Ads.** The Android version shows ads through Unity LevelPlay. The ads come from two ad networks run by Unity Technologies: ironSource Ads and Unity Ads.
- **What they collect.** To show ads and keep them safe, these services collect your device's advertising ID and other identifiers, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), information about the ads you see and tap, and technical diagnostics. The details are below.
- **Your choice.** When you first start the game, before any ads load, it asks whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Personalised ads. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
- **Nothing for us.** We never ask for your name, your email or an account. The game sends no data to us, and it has no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
- **Offline.** The game works without an internet connection. When you are offline, no ads are shown and nothing can be sent.
- **On your device.** Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
- **Windows.** The Windows version has no ads and sends no data.

## Ads in the game

The game shows a banner in its menus, a full-screen ad between missions from time to time, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus, such as extra coins.

LevelPlay is a mediation service from Unity Technologies (ironSource is part of Unity). When the game needs an ad, LevelPlay asks the ad networks we have enabled and shows the best offer. These are the networks enabled for the game:

| Ad network | Operated by | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| ironSource Ads (through LevelPlay) | Unity Technologies | [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) |
| Unity Ads | Unity Technologies | [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) |

Unity's general [Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) also applies. Before an ad network from another company shows any ads in the game, we will list it here.

## Information collected for ads

LevelPlay and the ad networks collect this information from your device:

| Information | What it means | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising ID and other identifiers | Your device's advertising ID, plus identifiers that the ad services give your device, the game installation or an ad session | Showing and counting ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ads, preventing fraud, and (only if you agree) personalising ads |
| Approximate location | Worked out from your IP address: your country, region or city. The game never uses GPS or your precise location | Choosing ads that suit your region, following local laws, preventing fraud |
| Ad interactions and app use | Which ads were shown, and whether you tapped one or watched a rewarded ad to the end. Unity Ads may also record when and for how long the game is used | Giving you rewards, measuring ads and their results, preventing fraud |
| Diagnostics | Technical information about your device and about how the ads perform, such as the device model, the Android version and loading errors | Keeping ads working, analysing how ads perform, preventing fraud |

Unity Ads also lists "purchase history" among the data its software can collect. SKY RUSH sells nothing for real money: coins and colour schemes are bought only with coins earned in the game, and that information never leaves your device.

**What we receive.** LevelPlay and Unity give us reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned. The reports don't tell us your name or contact details, and we don't try to identify you.

## Personalised ads and your choices

- **The first-launch question.** Before the game starts the ad services for the first time, it asks whether you agree to personalised ads. Your answer is saved on your device. Each time you open the game, it applies your answer before the ad software starts.
- **If you choose Yes,** the ad networks may use the information above, together with information they have collected in other apps, to show ads that match your interests and to measure how well the ads work.
- **If you choose No,** the game tells LevelPlay that you do not consent to personalised ads and that you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (as US state privacy laws call it). LevelPlay passes this on to the ad networks. You still see ads, but they are chosen without a profile of you, for example from the game you are playing and your approximate location. The networks still use the information above to deliver each ad, count it, limit repeats and prevent fraud.
- **Change your mind at any time** in the game's Settings > Personalised ads. The new choice applies to the ads loaded after the change.
- **Your advertising ID.** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices: Settings > Google > Ads). After you delete it, apps receive only zeros instead of an ID.
- **Inside Unity Ads ads.** Ads from Unity Ads show a Data Privacy icon. It leads to Unity's own privacy choices, for example opting out of certain personalised ads.
- **Unity's opt-out page:** [Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information](https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information).

## Legal bases (EEA, UK and similar laws)

- **Personalised ads:** your consent. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Personalised ads.
- **Non-personalised ads, ad measurement, limiting repeats and fraud prevention:** the legitimate interests of us and our ad partners in offering a free game that ads pay for, and in keeping those ads safe and honest.

## Information stored on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves on your device:

- **Your progress:** coins, unlocked missions, upgrades, purchased colour schemes, your selected aircraft and colour scheme, and mission results such as best score, time and kills.
- **Your settings:** volume, controls, graphics and display options, and your answer to the personalised-ads question.

This data stays in the game's own storage on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. On Windows, it is in your user profile's local application data (`AppData\LocalLow`). The game also writes a technical log file (`Player.log`) there, which never leaves your computer either.

On Android, the ad software may also store information on your device, such as its identifiers, its settings and downloaded ads.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android may include the game's save in your device backup. That backup belongs to your Google Account and you control it. We never have access to it.

## Device features and permissions

- **Internet and network state (Android).** Used only to load ads. Nothing else in the game uses the internet.
- **Advertising ID (Android).** The `AD_ID` permission lets the ad software read your device's advertising ID.
- **Vibration.** The game can vibrate for feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings. For this, the Android version requests the vibration permission (`VIBRATE`).
- **Motion sensors.** Tilt steering is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, the game reads your device's motion sensors to steer the aircraft. The readings are used on your device while you play and are never stored or sent.
- **Not used:** the camera, the microphone, GPS or precise location, contacts, photos and files.

## Analytics and other third parties

The game has no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and Unity's engine diagnostics are switched off. Apart from the ad services above, the game shares no information with anyone.

When you download the game from Google Play, Google handles that download under its own privacy policy. Google Play also gives us aggregated crash and performance statistics for the game, which Google collects under its own privacy policy.

## Children

SKY RUSH is not directed at children under 13, and its ad services are set up for a general audience, not as child-directed. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has played the game with ads, contact us and we will help to have the ad data deleted.

## How long information is kept

We keep no information about you. Unity keeps ad data for as long as it needs it for the purposes described in its privacy policy, or as the law allows. Its policy has the details.

## Security and international transfers

Unity states that its ad software encrypts the data it sends. Unity Technologies is based in the United States and may process the information there and in other countries, as its privacy policy describes. Your save never leaves your device.

## Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy, to withdraw your consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information. In the EEA and the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

- **Data on your device:** uninstall the game or clear its storage. On Android, go to Settings > Apps > SKY RUSH > Storage > Clear storage.
- **Ad data:** we hold none, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Unity holds it, and you have two ways to reach it:
  - Ask Unity directly. Unity's [Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy) explains how to access or delete your data and how to contact Unity's privacy team.
  - Or write to us at gngames2020@gmail.com. We will pass your request on to Unity and help you with it. Please include your device's advertising ID (Settings > Privacy > Ads), because the ad data is linked to identifiers like it, not to your name.
- **Advertising ID:** you can also reset or delete it at any time, as described above.
