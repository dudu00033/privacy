# Privacy Policy for ONE CHANCE

**Last updated: 27 October 2026**

ONE CHANCE is a timing puzzle game published by CG Art Studio. This policy explains what the app
collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: the game itself collects nothing and has no account, no login and no server.
Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads, and the advertising software is what most
of this page is about.

## What the game stores

The levels you have unlocked and completed, your best time and number of attempts on each level,
your coin balance, the orb skins you own and the one you have equipped, the progress rewards you
have already collected, your hint tokens and the hints you have unlocked, today's daily challenge
(whether you have completed it, your best time, your attempts and your streak), simple totals such
as how many levels you have completed, when you last watched the shop's bonus-coins video (to keep
the five-minute wait between those offers), and your settings: music and sound effects on or off,
their volume, and vibration on or off.

All of it is written to one save file, plus a backup copy of it so that a crash mid-save can never
corrupt your progress, both in your device's own app storage. It never leaves the device. We cannot
read it, and both files are deleted when you uninstall the app.

There is no account to create, nothing to log into, and no way for us to tell one player from
another. The game sends no analytics and no crash reports of its own.

**There are no purchases in ONE CHANCE.** Coins are earned by playing and spent only on orb skins
and hints inside the game. Nothing is sold for money, so no payment information of any kind is
asked for, handled or stored.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: short haptic feedback for important moments in play. You can switch it off in
  the game's Settings. It involves no data of any kind.
- **Internet access** and **network state**: used only by the advertising software described
  below. The game itself works completely offline.
- **Advertising ID**: lets the advertising software read your device's advertising ID (see below).
  You control this ID in Android's settings.
- **Android ad services** (ad attribution and ad topics, part of Android's Privacy Sandbox): declared
  by the advertising software, so it can use Android's privacy-preserving ad measurement where the
  device supports it. You manage these in Android's ad privacy settings.
- **Keep awake**, **run at startup** and **foreground service**: declared by a standard Android
  background-task library that the advertising software includes, so it can finish small pending
  tasks such as delivering an ad report. The game itself schedules no background work and does
  nothing when you are not playing.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Your progress is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it stays
on the phone it was created on.

## Where ads appear

ONE CHANCE shows three kinds of ad, and **no ad ever appears while you are playing a level**:

- **Videos you choose to watch.** Some buttons offer a reward for watching a video: continue
  after a failed attempt, unlock a hint, double the coins from a cleared level, or collect bonus
  coins in the shop. The reward is given only if the video is watched to the end. Nothing happens
  if you don't press the button.
- **Full-screen ads between levels.** Occasionally, when you leave a result screen to go to the
  next level or back to a menu. Never when you press RETRY, never during a level, never in your
  first five levels, at most one every two minutes and every three level results, and never within
  two minutes of a video you chose to watch.
- **A banner** at the bottom of the main menu, the level list and the shop. Never on the game
  screen.

If no ad is available, or you are offline, the game carries on exactly the same. A missing ad
never costs you anything.

## What the advertising collects

Ads are served through **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource). It runs an auction among the advertising
networks configured for the app, currently **ironSource** and **Unity Ads**, and passes the
request to whichever of them wins. To select, deliver and measure ads, these companies may
collect:

- Your device's **advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID)
- **Identifiers the advertising software creates for this installation**, used to keep ad
  sessions consistent, cap how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. They are not linked to
  your name.
- Your **IP address**, from which an approximate location (typically your country) can be
  derived
- **Device information**: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language and screen size
- **Ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, how long they were watched, and whether they
  were tapped
- **Diagnostics**: performance and error information about the advertising software itself, used
  to keep it working and to detect fraud

Nothing the advertising software collects is used inside the game. The data is collected by
those companies directly, sent encrypted, and may be shared among the advertising partners taking
part in the auction, under their own policies:

- Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads):
  https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We do not receive any of it in a form that identifies you. What we see are aggregate counts:
how many ads were shown, and what they earned.

## Personalised advertising

ONE CHANCE requests **non-personalised** advertising. It tells the advertising software that no
consent to personalised ads has been given, and that your personal information may not be sold.
Ads are chosen without using a profile built from your past activity.

## Children

ONE CHANCE is **not directed to children under 13**, and the app is registered with its
advertising partners as not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If
you believe a child has used the app and you want their advertising data removed, contact us and
we will pass the request to our advertising partners.

## Your choices

**Skip the optional videos.** They play only when you press a button that offers one.

**Reset or delete your advertising ID.** On Android: *Settings → Privacy → Ads*. You can reset
the ID, which breaks the link to everything previously associated with it, or delete it
entirely, after which apps receive no ID at all.

**Opt out of personalised ads** in the same settings screen.

**Play offline.** With no connection, no ads are requested and none of the data above is sent.

**Uninstall the app.** This deletes everything ONE CHANCE stored on your device.

## How to request deletion of your data

This section is the one Google Play links to as the game's data deletion request page, and it
is written to be followed step by step.

**1. Delete what is on your device.** Uninstall ONE CHANCE (or clear its storage in Android
settings). Everything the game itself stored (progress, times, coins, skins, hints, daily
challenge and settings) is deleted with it, immediately and completely. There is no copy anywhere
else, because the game has no server.

**2. Break the link to your advertising data.** On Android, open *Settings → Privacy → Ads* and
choose **Delete advertising ID**, or **Reset advertising ID** if you would rather keep receiving
ads without the history.

**3. Ask the advertising partner to erase what it holds.** The advertising data is held by Unity
(LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), not by us. Request its deletion through the privacy
contact in their policy:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

**4. Or write to us and we will pass the request on.** gngames2020@gmail.com or
GiorgiAmirkhanovi@gmail.com. Include your
advertising ID from step 2. Without it there is no way to identify which records are yours,
because we hold nothing that names you.

### What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

| Data | Who holds it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progress, times, coins, skins, hints, daily challenge, settings | Your device only | Deleted the moment you uninstall. Never transmitted. |
| Advertising ID | Your device, and Unity | You delete or reset it yourself, at any time, in Android settings. |
| Approximate location, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics | Unity / ironSource | Retained under Unity's own policy. Deletion is requested through them, or through us. |
| Aggregate counts: how many ads were shown and what they earned | Us | Kept indefinitely. These are totals, not records about you, so there is nothing in them to delete. |
