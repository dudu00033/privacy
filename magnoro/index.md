# Privacy Policy for Magnoro

**Last updated: 11 September 2026**

Magnoro is a puzzle game published by GNGames2020. This policy explains what the app
collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: the game itself collects nothing and has no account, no login and no
server. Everything it remembers about you stays on your phone. The advertising inside it
is a different matter, and that is what most of this page is about.

## What the game stores

Your level, your coins, your stars, your daily-puzzle streak, the language you picked, and
whether sound, music, vibration and colourblind shapes are on. All of it is written to a
single file in your device's own app storage and never leaves it. We cannot read it, and it
is deleted when you uninstall the app.

There is no account to create, nothing to log into, and no way for us to tell one player
from another.

**There are no purchases in Magnoro.** Nothing in the game is sold for money, so no payment
information of any kind is asked for, handled or stored.

## What the advertising collects

Magnoro shows ads through **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource), which runs an auction among the
advertising networks configured for the app and passes each request to whichever of them
wins it. There are three places an ad can appear: a banner strip along the bottom of the
screen, a full-screen ad between levels, and a video you choose to watch in exchange for a
hint, an extra heart or coins.

To select, deliver and measure ads, these companies may collect:

- Your device's **advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID)
- Your **IP address**, from which an approximate location — typically your country — can
  be derived
- **Device information**: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language,
  screen size, and whether the device is a tablet or a phone
- **Ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, how long they were watched, and whether
  they were clicked
- **Diagnostics**: performance and error information about the advertising software itself,
  used to keep it working and to detect fraud

This data is collected by those companies directly, under their own policies:

- Unity (LevelPlay and ironSource):
  https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We do not receive any of it in a form that identifies you. What we see are aggregate
counts — how many ads were shown, and what they earned.

## Personalised advertising

By default, Magnoro requests **non-personalised** advertising. Ads are chosen without using
a profile built from your past activity.

## Children

Magnoro is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their advertising data
removed, contact us and we will pass the request to our advertising partners.

## Your choices

**Reset or delete your advertising ID.** On Android: *Settings → Privacy → Ads*. You can
reset the ID, which breaks the link to everything previously associated with it, or delete
it entirely, after which apps receive no ID at all.

**Opt out of personalised ads** in the same settings screen.

**Uninstall the app.** This deletes everything Magnoro stored on your device.

To ask what an advertising partner holds about your device, or to have it deleted, contact
Unity through the policy linked above — they are the party that holds it.

## How to request deletion of your data

This section is the one Google Play links to as Magnoro's data deletion request page, and it
is written to be followed step by step.

**1. Delete what is on your device.** Uninstall Magnoro. Everything the game itself stored —
your level, coins, stars, streak, language and settings — is deleted with it, immediately and
completely. There is no copy anywhere else, because the game has no server.

**2. Break the link to your advertising data.** On Android, open *Settings → Privacy → Ads*
and choose **Delete advertising ID**, or **Reset advertising ID** if you would rather keep
receiving ads without the history. Deleting it stops apps receiving an ID at all; resetting
it issues a new one and severs everything associated with the old.

**3. Ask the advertising partner to erase what it holds.** The advertising data is held by
Unity (LevelPlay and ironSource), not by us. Request its deletion through the privacy contact
in their policy:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

**4. Or write to us and we will pass the request on.** GNGames2020@gmail.com. Include your
advertising ID from step 2 — without it there is no way to identify which records are yours,
because we hold nothing that names you.

### What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

| Data | Who holds it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level, coins, stars, streak, settings | Your device only | Deleted the moment you uninstall. Never transmitted. |
| Advertising ID | Your device, and Unity | You delete or reset it yourself, at any time, in Android settings. |
| Approximate location, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics | Unity / ironSource | Retained under Unity's own policy. Deletion is requested through them, or through us. |
| Aggregate counts — how many ads were shown and what they earned | Us | Kept indefinitely. **These are totals, not records about you**: they contain no identifier and cannot be traced back to any device, so there is nothing in them to delete. |

We keep no account, no profile and no identifier of any kind. There is no record of you here
to erase — which is why every step above points at your device or at Unity.
