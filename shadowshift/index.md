# Privacy Policy for SHADOW SHIFT

**Last updated: 30 October 2026**

SHADOW SHIFT is a 3D light-and-shadow puzzle game published by CG Art Studio. This policy explains
what the app collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: the game itself collects nothing and has no account, no login and no
server. Everything it remembers about you stays on your phone. The advertising inside it is a
different matter, and that is what most of this page is about.

## What the game stores

Your progress through the 50 levels (which levels and worlds are unlocked and completed, your best
star rating, best time and best move count for each level, and how many times you have completed
it), your coin, gem and hint balances, the character skins, light themes and trails you own and
the ones you have equipped, your mission progress, your daily reward progress (which day you are
on and the date you last claimed), which tutorial tips you have already seen, the world and level
you played last, simple totals such as how many levels you have completed and how many hints you
have used, and your settings: music and sound volume, vibration on or off, graphics quality and
language. The file also records when it was created and when it was last saved.

All of it is written to your device's own app storage and never leaves it through the game. We
cannot read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

To suggest a graphics quality the first time you play, the game looks at how much memory and how
many processor cores your phone has. Only the chosen quality level is kept; the figures themselves
are not stored or sent anywhere.

There is no account to create, nothing to log into, and no way for us to tell one player from
another. The game sends no analytics and no crash reports of its own: Unity's analytics, crash
reporting and diagnostics are all switched off in this app.

**There are no purchases in SHADOW SHIFT.** Coins and gems are earned by playing and spent only
on looks, hints and similar items inside the game. Nothing is sold for money, so no payment
information of any kind is asked for, handled or stored.

### Device backup

If you have switched on your phone's own backup to your Google account, Android may include the
game's save in that backup and restore it when you set up a new phone or reinstall. That backup
goes to your Google account, not to us, and is controlled by your phone's backup settings.

## Permissions

- **Vibration**: for short haptic feedback during play. You can switch it off in the game's
  Settings. It involves no data of any kind.
- **Internet access and network state**: used only by the advertising software described below.
  The game itself can be played completely offline.
- **Advertising ID and Android's ad services (attribution and topics)**: used only by the
  advertising software described below.
- **Wake lock, foreground service and start-at-boot**: requested by the advertising software so it
  can finish its own background tasks, such as sending queued ad reports. The game does not use them.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## What the advertising collects

SHADOW SHIFT shows three kinds of ad, and **none of them ever interrupts a puzzle**:

- a small **banner** at the bottom of the menu screens (main menu, world and level selection, shop,
  missions, daily reward and settings), never during a level;
- an occasional **full-screen ad between levels**. It never appears in the first levels of the game,
  never more than once every few completed levels and never within a few minutes of the previous
  full-screen ad. It only appears after you have finished a level and tapped to move on.
- a **video you choose to watch**: either for a hint when you have none left, in which case the level
  is paused while the video plays, or on the results screen to double the coins you just earned.

The ads are served through **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource), which runs an auction between the
advertising networks set up for the app and passes each request to whichever of them wins. In
SHADOW SHIFT those networks are **ironSource Ads** and **Unity Ads**. To select, deliver and
measure ads, these companies may collect:

- Your device's **advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID) and **app set ID**
- **Identifiers the advertising software creates for this installation**: a random user ID used to
  keep ad sessions consistent, cap how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud. It is not linked to
  your name.
- Your **IP address**, from which an approximate location (typically your country) can be
  derived
- **Device information**: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, screen size,
  and whether the device is a tablet or a phone
- **Ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, how long they were watched, taps and page views
  inside the ad itself (never during gameplay), and whether they were clicked
- **Diagnostics**: performance and error information about the advertising software itself, used
  to keep it working and to detect fraud

This data is collected by those companies directly and may be shared among the advertising partners
taking part in the auction, under their own policies:

- Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads):
  https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We do not receive any of it in a form that identifies you. What we see are aggregate counts:
how many ads were shown, and what they earned.

## Personalised advertising

SHADOW SHIFT requests **non-personalised** advertising. Before the advertising software starts, the
app tells it that you have not given consent for personalised ads, that your data must not be sold or
shared ("do not sell or share"), and that the app is not directed to children. Ads are chosen
without using a profile built from your past activity.

## Children

SHADOW SHIFT is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their advertising data removed,
contact us and we will pass the request to our advertising partners.

## Your choices

**Reset or delete your advertising ID.** On Android: *Settings → Privacy → Ads*. You can reset
the ID, which breaks the link to everything previously associated with it, or delete it
entirely, after which apps receive no ID at all.

**Opt out of personalised ads** in the same settings screen.

**Skip the videos.** The hint and coin-doubling videos appear only when you press the button that
offers them.

**Start over.** *Settings → Reset progress* in the game clears your progress, balances and unlocks
(your settings are kept).

**Uninstall the app.** This deletes everything SHADOW SHIFT stored on your device.

To ask what an advertising partner holds about your device, or to have it deleted, contact Unity
through the policy linked above. They are the party that holds it.

## How to request deletion of your data

This section is the one Google Play links to as the game's data deletion request page, and it
is written to be followed step by step.

**1. Delete what is on your device.** Uninstall SHADOW SHIFT (or clear its storage in Android
settings). Everything the game itself stored, such as level progress, stars, coins, gems, hints,
skins, light themes, trails, missions, daily rewards and settings, is deleted with it, immediately
and completely. We hold no copy, because the game has no server. (If your phone backs up apps to
your own Google account, delete the backup there too.)

**2. Break the link to your advertising data.** On Android, open *Settings → Privacy → Ads* and
choose **Delete advertising ID**, or **Reset advertising ID** if you would rather keep receiving
ads without the history. Deleting it stops apps receiving an ID at all; resetting it issues a new
one and severs everything associated with the old.

**3. Ask the advertising partner to erase what it holds.** The advertising data is held by Unity
(LevelPlay, ironSource and Unity Ads), not by us. Request its deletion through the privacy
contact in their policy:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

**4. Or write to us and we will pass the request on.** GiorgiAmirkhanovi@gmail.com. Include your
advertising ID from step 2. Without it there is no way to identify which records are yours,
because we hold nothing that names you.

### What is deleted, what is kept, and for how long

| Data | Who holds it | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level progress, stars, coins, gems, hints, skins, missions, daily rewards, settings | Your device only (and your own Google backup, if you use it) | Deleted the moment you uninstall. Never sent to us. |
| Advertising ID | Your device, and Unity | You delete or reset it yourself, at any time, in Android settings. |
| Approximate location, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics | Unity / ironSource | Retained under Unity's own policy. Deletion is requested through them, or through us. |
| Aggregate counts: how many ads were shown and what they earned | Us | Kept indefinitely. **These are totals, not records about you**: they contain no identifier and cannot be traced back to any device, so there is nothing in them to delete. |

We keep no account, no profile and no identifier of any kind. There is no record of you here to
erase, which is why every step above points at your device or at Unity.
