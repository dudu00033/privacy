# Privacy Policy for Nockle

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

Nockle is a puzzle game published by GNGames2020. This policy explains what the app
collects, who receives it, and what you can do about it.

The short version: the game itself collects nothing and has no account, no login and no
server. Everything it remembers about you stays on your phone. The advertising inside it
is a different matter, and that is what most of this page is about.

## What the game stores

Your level, which colour themes you have unlocked, which one you picked, and whether the
sound is on. All of it is written to your device's own local storage and never leaves it.
We cannot read it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

There is no account to create, nothing to log into, and no way for us to tell one player
from another.

## What the advertising collects

Nockle shows ads through **Unity LevelPlay** (ironSource), which runs an auction among the
advertising networks configured for the app and passes each request to whichever of them
wins it. To select, deliver and measure ads, these companies may collect:

- Your device's **advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID)
- Your **IP address**, from which an approximate location — typically your country — can
  be derived
- **Device information**: model, manufacturer, operating system version, language,
  screen size, and whether the device is a tablet or a phone
- **Ad interaction data**: which ads were shown, how long they were watched, and whether
  they were clicked
- **Diagnostics**: performance and error information about the advertising software itself,
  used to keep it working and to detect fraud

This data is collected by those companies directly, under their own policies:

- Unity (LevelPlay and ironSource):
  https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We do not receive any of it in a form that identifies you. What we see are aggregate
counts — how many ads were shown, and what they earned.

## Personalised advertising

By default, Nockle requests **non-personalised** advertising. Ads are chosen without
using a profile built from your past activity.

## Children

Nockle is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their advertising data
removed, contact us and we will pass the request to our advertising partners.

## Your choices

**Reset or delete your advertising ID.** On Android:
*Settings → Privacy → Ads*. You can reset the ID, which breaks the link to everything
previously associated with it, or delete it entirely, after which apps receive no ID at
all.

**Opt out of personalised ads** in the same settings screen.

**Uninstall the app.** This deletes everything Nockle stored on your device.

To ask what an advertising partner holds about your device, or to have it deleted,
contact Unity through the policy linked above — they are the party that holds it.
